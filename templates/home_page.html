What exactly is Career Traces?
Career Traces is a job application tracker. You apply for jobs through LinkedIn, company portals, etc. — then log and manage those applications here. Your personal job search command center.
Is it actually free? No catch?
Yes, 100% free right now. All features — tracking, analytics, interview scheduler — are completely free. We may introduce premium features in the future, but existing access won't be taken away.
Can I find job listings on Career Traces?
No — Career Traces is not a job board. It's purely a tracker. You find jobs elsewhere and log them here to stay organized.
How do I reset my password?
Use the "Forgot Password" option on the login page. A one-time password (OTP) is sent to your registered email. Takes under a minute.
Is my data private?
Absolutely. Your data is securely stored and only accessible by you. We don't sell or share it with any third parties.
Is Career Traces mobile-friendly?
Yes — fully responsive and works seamlessly on desktop, tablet, and mobile devices.
When will data export be available?
Export to CSV and PDF is in active development. We'll notify all users when it launches.
